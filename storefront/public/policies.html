CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Store policies.

These policy sections are publication placeholders, not legal terms. Before accepting orders, replace each section with the business's approved policy and confirm that the store's checkout activation settings reflect that review.

Shipping policy

Publish shipping regions, rates, handling times, carrier methods, delivery estimates, lost/damaged parcel procedure, and a customer contact address before enabling checkout.

Returns and refunds

Publish the return window, condition requirements, exclusions, return shipping responsibility, refund timing, cancellation process, and contact details. Do not assume a statutory exemption.

Privacy notice

Explain what customer information is collected at checkout, that Stripe processes payment information, what order information is retained, lawful purposes, retention periods, customer rights, and how to contact the business about privacy.

Terms of sale

Publish the legal seller identity and contact information, pricing and currency, product descriptions, order acceptance/cancellation rules, taxes, delivery, risk of loss, warranties, governing law, and dispute/contact process.
